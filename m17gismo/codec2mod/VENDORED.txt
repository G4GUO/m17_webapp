This fork only implements the 3200 bps mode (8 bytes / 160 samples per frame),
which is what M17 uses. It has no build system of its own, so the sources are
compiled directly by the m17web makefile (CODEC2_BACKEND=mod) through the small
upstream-compatible shim in codec2_shim.c.
